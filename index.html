Journal Publication

MediEdge: A Low-Cost Edge-Based Cyber-Physical IoT Platform for Real-Time Remote Patient Monitoring with Comparative Validation
Measurement (Elsevier), Vol. 292, 2026, Article 123099.
Ajoy Shil (First Author), Joy Mohajan, Akib Jayed Islam.
DOI: 10.1016/j.measurement.2026.123099
  • Published in a Q1 journal (Impact Factor: 5.6), Ranked in Top 10% in Instrumentation & Measurement.
  • Developed a low-cost ESP32-based edge-first IoT platform for multimodal physiological monitoring (ECG, PPG/SpO2, body temperature, ambient sensing).
  • Implemented embedded signal processing: baseline stabilization, cascaded IIR filtering, QRS detection, and confidence-aware SpO2 stabilization.
  • Validated with 2000 paired prototype-reference observations: HR MAE = 2.88 bpm (ICC = 0.961), SpO2 MAE = 2.12% (ICC = 0.786).
  • Demonstrated edge resilience under intermittent connectivity with 98.7% cloud synchronization success and 12.6 h battery operation.
