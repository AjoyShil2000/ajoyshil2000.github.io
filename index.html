About

Ajoy Shil is a researcher and embedded-systems developer working at the intersection of biomedical engineering, Internet of Things (IoT), edge computing, and remote health monitoring. His work focuses on accessible, low-cost systems that connect physiological sensing with practical digital health applications.

Education: B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Port City International University, Bangladesh.

Featured Research: MediEdge

MediEdge: A Low-Cost Edge-Based Cyber-Physical IoT Platform for Real-Time Remote Patient Monitoring with Comparative Validation

MediEdge is a research platform for remote patient monitoring that integrates physiological sensors, embedded processing, and secure IoT data communication. The project explores practical, affordable approaches to connected healthcare and comparative validation of physiological measurements.
